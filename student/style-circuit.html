Move each bit, then fill empty places with 0

A logical shift left by 1 doubles a number (×2). A logical shift right by 1 halves it (÷2) when it divides exactly. Each extra place doubles or halves again. Practise shifts of up to 6 places; every answer fits in 8 bits and uses a whole number.

When is a shift useful? To double a game score or halve an even quantity, use one place. To multiply or divide by 4, use two places. Bits moved off the edge are discarded; empty positions become 0.
